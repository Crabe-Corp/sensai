# Eastern Zhou Strategies

## Eastern Zhou


### State of Qin Mobilizes an Army to Approach Zhou and Demands the Nine Tripods

The State of Qin mobilized its army to approach Zhou and demanded the Nine Tripods; King Zhou was worried about this, and informed Yan Lüe. Yan Lüe said: "Your Majesty need not worry. I shall go east to request aid from Qi." Yan Lüe went to Qi and told King Qi: "The State of Qin has been acting without morality, wishing to mobilize its army against Zhou in order to demand the Nine Tripods. The king and ministers of Zhou have considered all possible measures internally; giving it to Qin would be worse than giving it to your great state." Preserving a perilous state is a beautiful reputation; obtaining the Nine Tripods would be acquiring a precious treasure. I hope Your Majesty will consider this." King Qi was greatly pleased, and mobilized 50,000 troops, appointing Chen Chensi to lead them in rescuing Zhou, and the Qin army withdrew.

The general of Qi requested the Nine Tripods, and King Zhou was troubled once again. Yan Lüe said: "Your Majesty need not worry; I shall go east to resolve this matter." Yan Lüe arrived in Qi and told King Qi: "Zhou relies on the righteousness of your great state to preserve its king, ministers, fathers, and sons. We wish to present the Nine Tripods; but I wonder which route Your Majesty would take to bring them to Qi?" King Qi said: "I shall send it through a shortcut via Liang." Yan Lüe replied: "No. The king and ministers of Liang have long desired the Nine Tripods, plotting about it under the Hui Tai pavilion and above the Shao Hai lake for a long time. Once the tripods enter Liang, they will surely never leave." King Qi said: "I shall send it via a shortcut through Chu." Yan Lüe replied: "No. The king and ministers of Chu have long desired the Nine Tripords, plotting about it in the Ye Ting courtyard for a long time. If they enter Chu, the tripods will surely never leave." King Qi asked: "Ultimately, which route should I take to bring them to my state of Qi?" Yan Lüe said: "My humble state has secretly been concerned for Your Majesty. The Nine Tripods are not like small earthenware jars or sauce bottles that can be carried by the hand and brought to Qi. They are not like flocks of birds, flying crows, leaping hares, or galloping horses that can suddenly stop in Qi. In the past, when Zhou attacked Yin and obtained the Nine Tripods, it took 90,000 men to haul each tripod; for nine tripods, that would be 810,000 people. Soldiers, troops, weapons, equipment, and supplies required were all proportionate to this number. Now, even if Your Majesty has these people, which route would you take to transport them? I secretly worry about this for Your Majesty." King Qi said: "Your repeated visits are as if they have not happened at all." Yan Lüe replied: "I dare not deceive your great state. Please quickly decide the route to take, and my humble state will move the tripods in readiness for orders." King Qi then ceased his request.

### Qin Attacks Yiyang

The State of Qin attacked Yiyang, and King Zhou said to Zhao Lei, "What do you think of this?" Zhao Lei replied, "Yiyang will certainly be captured." The king said, "Yiyang's city is eight li square, with ten thousand capable soldiers and grain reserves sufficient for several years. General Gongzhong has an army of twenty thousand men, and Jing Cui commands the large forces of Chu to come to its rescue from the mountains. Qin will surely achieve no success." Zhao Lei replied, "Gan Mao is a foreigner in service. If he attacks Yiyang and achieves success, then he will be as illustrious as the Duke of Zhou." "If he fails to achieve success, his name will be erased from Qin's records." King of Qin did not heed the advice of ministers, fathers, and elder brothers but attacked Yiyang. If Yiyang is not captured, King of Qin will consider it a disgrace. "Therefore I said that Yiyang will be captured." The king said, "What strategy do you suggest for me?" Zhao Lei replied, "Your Majesty should tell Jing Cui, 'You hold the rank of Zhigu, and your official title is Zhu Guo. If you win a battle, there can be no greater reward than this; if you lose, then death awaits. It would be better for you to abandon Qin and support Yiyang by sending your troops forward.'" Qin will fear that you might take advantage of their weakness, so they must offer treasures to appease you; and Gongzhong, who hopes for your assistance against Qin on his behalf, will also certainly give you all his treasures.'" Qin captured Yiyang, and as a result Jing Cui indeed advanced his troops. Fearing this development, Qin quickly offered the town of Zhu Zao; and indeed, the State of Han also presented valuable treasures. Jing Cui obtained a city from Qin and received treasures from Han, thus becoming indebted to Eastern Zhou.

### Eastern Zhou and Western Zhou Fight

The Eastern Zhou and Western Zhou fought against each other, and the State of Han came to the aid of the Western Zhou. On behalf of the Eastern Zhou, someone told King of Han: "The Western Zhou is an ancient state of the Son of Heaven; it has many famous ritual vessels and precious treasures. Keep your troops in readiness and do not engage, and you can gain favor with the Eastern Zhou; all the treasures of the Western Zhou will then fall into your hands."


### Eastern Zhou and the Western Zhou Contend

The Eastern Zhou and Western Zhou contended with each other; the Western Zhou wanted to make peace with the states of Chu and Han. Qi Ming said to Lord of Eastern Zhou, "I am afraid that the Western Zhou will form an alliance with Chu and Han, causing them to request territory from the Eastern Zhou on their behalf. "It would be better for you to tell Chu and Han: 'The Western Zhou's desire to accept treasures is indecisive; they hold two positions. Now, if the Eastern Zhou does not urgently assist the Western Zhou, then the treasures of the Western Zhou will not enter Chu and Han.'" Chu and Han desire to obtain the treasures; they will therefore urge us to attack the Western Zhou. Once the treasures of the Western Zhou are taken out, it would mean that we have obtained the treasures for Chu and Han in a virtuous manner; thus, the Western Zhou will be weakened."


### Dongzhou Wants to Grow Rice

Dongzhou wanted to grow rice, but Xizhou would not let water flow down, and Dongzhou was worried about this. Su Zi said to the ruler of Dongzhou: "May I request permission to make Xizhou let water flow down?" He went to see the ruler of Xizhou and said, "Your plan is wrong! Not letting water flow down now is precisely what enriches Dongzhou. Now all their people are planting wheat, and there are no other crops left. If you wish to harm them, it would be better to let water flow down once so as to damage what they have planted." Letting the water flow down, Dongzhou will certainly replant rice; After planting rice and then taking it away again. If that is done, then the people of Dongzhou can be made to rely entirely on Xizhou and accept commands from you." The ruler of Xizhou said, "Good." So he let the water flow down. Su Zi also received gold from both states.


### Zhaoxian Was in Yangzhi

Zhaoxian was in Yangzhi. Zhou Jun was going to order the prime minister to go, but the prime minister did not want to. Su Li said on his behalf to Zhou Jun, "When King Chu met with King Wei, Your Majesty ordered Chen Feng to go to Chu and ordered Xiang Gong to go to Wei; when the meeting between Chu and Han took place, Your Majesty ordered Xu Gong to go to Chu and Xiang Gong to go to Han. Now Zhaoxian is not a ruler, yet Your Majesty orders the prime minister to go. If his king were in Yangzhi, would Your Majesty order someone else to go?" Zhou Jun said, "You are right." He then stopped the journey.


### Qin Borrows a Passage through Zhou to Attack Han

The State of Qin requested passage through Zhou territory to attack Han. Zhou was afraid that if it granted the request, Han would become hostile; but if it refused, Qin would become hostile. Shi Shu said to Duke Zhou: "Why does Your Majesty not have someone tell the Lord Gongshu of Han, 'Qin dares to cut off the pass and attack Han because it trusts Dongzhou.'" "Why don't you give Zhou some land, send a distinguished envoy to the State of Chu, and Qin will certainly become suspicious. If they no longer trust Zhou, Han will not be attacked.'" He also said to the King of Qin: "The State of Han is offering strong support and giving land to Zhou, intending to sow doubts about Zhou in your mind. I dare not refuse it." "Qin will certainly have no grounds to order Zhou to refuse, and thus you would gain land from Han while being obedient to Qin."


### Chu Attacks Yongshi

Chu attacked Yongshi, and Zhou supplied Qin and Han with provisions; King of Chu was angry at Zhou, and the ruler of Zhou was worried about this. A person on behalf of Zhou said to King of Chu, "With Your Majesty's strength and anger toward Zhou, Zhou will be afraid. It must ally its country with the state that provided it grain; this would strengthen Your Majesty's enemies." "Therefore, Your Majesty had better quickly relieve Zhou's fear. If they first committed an offense and then received a resolution, they will certainly serve Your Majesty with great devotion."


### Zhou Zui Advises Shi Li

Zhou Zui said to Shi Li, "Why don't you use Qin to attack Qi?" "I will ask the prime minister of Qi to be your son. If you serve Qin with Qi, there is certainly no place for you." "You should then order Zhou Zui to reside in Wei so that they can support each other; thus, the whole world will be under your control." "You would hold great influence in the east at Qi and high esteem in the west at Qin. If Qin and Qi unite, then you will always be of great importance."


### Zhou Prime Minister Lü Cang Receives Guests at the Zhou Ruler's Court

The Zhou prime minister Lü Cang received guests at the Zhou ruler's court. The former prime minister Gongshi Ji, fearing that the guest would harm him, therefore had someone tell the Zhou ruler: "This guest is an eloquent man; however, the reason why he cannot be accepted is because he likes to slander others."


### King Wen of Zhou Dismisses Shi Gong Shi Ji

Zhou Wenjun dismissed Shigong Shi Ji, and appointed Xiang Lücang as prime minister; the people of the state were not pleased. The ruler had a kind-hearted mind. He said to Zhou Wenjun, "A state must have both criticism and praise; loyal ministers ensure that the criticism falls upon themselves while the praise goes to the ruler." King Song seized time from the people to build a terrace, yet did not consider it as coming from the people; there were no loyal ministers to cover up for him. Zi Han relinquished his position as prime minister and became Sikong instead, and the people criticized Zi Han but praised their ruler; King Huan of Qi had seven markets in his palace and 700 concubines, and the people criticized him. Guan Zhong therefore became a man of the San Gui family, to cover for King Huan; this was not an act that harmed himself among the people. The "Spring and Autumn Annals" records hundreds of cases where ministers killed their rulers, and in all these cases, the ministers were those who had been praised by high-ranking officials. Therefore, when a high minister receives praise, it is not a good thing for the state. Thus, many common people become strong, and accumulated contributions form a mountain." King Zhou was thus inevitably unable to avoid it.


### Wen Renzhi Zhou

Wen Renzhi Zhou, Zhou bu na. "Ke ji?" Dui yue: "Zhu ren ye." They asked about the alley and did not know, so they ordered him to be imprisoned. The ruler sent someone to ask him: "You are not a native of Zhou, yet why do you call yourself not a guest?" He replied: "Since my youth I have recited the Book of Songs, which says: 'Under the entire heavens, nothing is not the king's land; All along the lands, none are not the king's subjects.'" Now that Zhou is ruler of all under heaven, I am a subject of the Son of Heaven; how can I be considered a guest? Therefore, I said 'host.'" The ruler then ordered the officials to release him.


### Someone on behalf of Zhou Zui advised Jin Tou

Someone said to Jin Tou on behalf of Zhou Zui, "The State of Qin suspects the whole world because of Zhou Zui's alliance with Qi, and also knows that Zhao will find it difficult to fight against the people of Qi. Fearing a union between Qi and Han, Qin must first form an alliance with Qin." If Qin and Qi unite, then your state will be left vulnerable. You would do better to come to the aid of Qi, and thus assist Qin in attacking Han and Wei. Shangdang and Zhangzi will then belong to Zhao. You can obtain treasures from the east in Qin, acquire territory from the south in Han and Wei, and by proceeding gradually toward the east, you will eventually form an alliance."


### Zhou Zui Advises Jin Tou

Zhou Zui said to Jin Tou, "You have ordered Qín to fight against powerful Qi." If Qin wins the battle, it will seize territory from Qi and grant you fiefs, allowing you to make few territorial concessions while letting all under heaven engage in warfare; If Qin loses, the state will suffer great damage and have no choice but to submit to Qin. Qin has already occupied Shangdang and Taiyuan of the entire states of Han and Wei, extending westward as far as Qin's existing territory. The land of Qin constitutes half of the world, controlling the fate of Qi, Chu, and the Three Jin states. If your state is to be restored yet you yourself are in danger, what kind of strategy is this?"


### Shi Xingqin Advises Daliang Zao

Shi Xingqin said to Daliang Zao, "If you want to decide the name of a hegemon or overlord, it is better to prepare scholars from both Zhou states who are eloquent and knowledgeable." He said to Duke Zhou, "You would be better off sending eloquent and knowledgeable scholars to argue on your behalf with the State of Qin."


### To Xue Gong

He said to Xue Gong, "Zhou Zui was favored by King Qi but he was driven away. Listening to Zhu Fu and appointing Lu Li as chancellor is an attempt to gain favor with Qin." If Qin and Qi form an alliance, then Zhu Fu and Lu Li will be very powerful. If Zhou Zui is in Qi, Qin will surely look down on you. You would be better off quickly sending troops north, moving toward Zhao with Qin and Wei, to recruit Zhou Zui as a reserve force. This will not only restore King Qi's trust in you but also prevent other states from following suit. "If Qi does not ally with Qin, the other states will certainly follow Zhu Fu's lead and leave. Where else can he go? Who would King Qi rely on to govern his state?"


### Qi Tingtzhufu

Qi Tingzhufu, wai zhou zui. He said to King Qi, "Those who drive out Zhou Zui, listen to Zhu Fu, and appoint Lü Li as prime minister desire to gain deeply from Qin. If Qin gains control of the world, then it will launch a deep attack on Qi. When Qi unites, Zhao fears an attack; therefore, Zhao urgently sends troops to demonstrate strength to Qin. If Qin uses Zhao's aggression as a pretext and allies with Qi to attack Zhao, the underlying logic is the same; it certainly will not be accepted. Therefore, employing Zhu Fu follows the principle of governance in the world."


### Su Li for Zhou Zui Advises Su Qin

Su Li, speaking on behalf of Zhou Zui to Su Qin, said: "You would be better off having the king listen to Zui and make a territorial agreement with Wei. As a result, Zhao will certainly become angry. If you then ally with Qi, this means that you are aligning Qi with the powerful state of Chu." The official's son Jun said: "If you wish to take advantage of Zui's matter, then it is you who unites with Qi;" "the one who cedes territory will be Zui."


### To Zhou Zui: Chou He's Serving as Prime Minister of Song

He said to Zhou Zui: "Chou He's serving as prime minister of Song was intended to observe how Qin would respond to Zhao and Song, thereby defeating the three states." "If the three states are not defeated, they will strengthen Zhao and Song in the east to isolate Qin." "He also intended to observe how Han and Wei would respond to Qin. If they are not firm, then he will ally with Song to defeat the three states;" "then sell Zhao and Song to the three states." "Why don't you have someone tell the kings of Han and Wei: 'Do you want Qin and Zhao to betray each other?" "Why not have Zhou Zui serve as joint prime minister with you, making it appear that your positions cannot be separated? Then Qin and Zhao will certainly betray each other in order to align with Your Majesty."


### For Zhou Zui: Advising King Wei of Wei

Zhou Zui said to King Wei, "The State of Qin knows that Zhao will find it difficult to fight Qi. It fears the alliance between Qi and Zhao, so it must secretly support them." Zhao dares not to fight because it fears that Qin will not take action against it. Therefore, Zhao first forms an alliance with Qi. It is unacceptable for the State of Wei to remain inactive while Qin and Zhao compete over Qi. "If King Wei does not send Zhou Zui to form an alliance with Qi and instead attacks Qi urgently while the situation is still unsettled, it will be without justification."


### Saying to Zhou Zui: The King of Wei Gives His State to You

He said to Zhou Zui: "The King of Wei has given his state to you, and it is honorable for you to ally with Qin in order to attack Qi." Xue Gong is your former lord. To lightly forget his state of Xue, to disregard the ancestral tombs of his late sovereigns, yet you alone cultivate empty trust, perform noble deeds, clarify the officials, uphold your former lord, and refuse to participate in attacking Qi out of anger toward a powerful Qin—it is not acceptable. You would be better off saying to the King of Wei and Xue Gong: "Please allow me to enter Qi on behalf of Your Majesty; no power in the world will be able to harm Qi." And if there is any change, I shall request to rescue it; If there is no change, Your Majesty may then proceed to attack it." Moreover, I am a servant of Qi; if my actions were to burden the King's relations with all under heaven, that would not be acceptable. Your Majesty has shown me great favor; if I enter Qi, then Your Majesty will also be free from any entanglements with Qi."


### Zhao Takes Zhou's Jidi Territory

Zhao took Zhou's jidi territory, and the Zhou ruler was worried about this, so he reported it to the Zheng court. The Zheng court said: "Your Majesty need not worry; I shall use thirty gold pieces to recover it for you." The Zhou ruler gave him the money, and Zheng Chao presented it to Zhao's Grand Diviner, thus informing him about the jidi matter. When the king fell ill, he sent someone to consult the diviners. The Grand Diviner blamed it and said: "It is a spirit from Zhou's jidi that is causing trouble." Zhao then returned it.


### Du He Wants to Recommend Jing Cui to Zhou

Du He wanted to recommend Jing Cui to the Zhou state, and said to Duke Zhou: "Your country is small; if you exhaust your noble treasures and jade in order to serve the feudal lords, you cannot fail to be cautious." It is like a person setting up a net; if he sets it in a place with no birds, then all day long he will catch nothing. If he sets it where there are many birds, the birds will be frightened away; one must set it in a place between where there are and are not birds, only then can one catch many birds. Now you will offer gifts to the great men; if they look down on you, if you give them to petty people, then those petty people cannot be relied upon for anything, and it will only waste your wealth. "You must give to the poor scholars of today, those who are not yet great men but may become so in the future; only then will you be able to achieve your desires."


### Crown Prince of Zhou Dies

The Crown Prince of Zhou died. He had five commoner sons, all of whom he loved, but there was no clear choice for succession. Sima Jian said to King Chu, "Why don't you bestow a fief upon Gongzi Jiu and request that he be made Crown Prince?" Zuo Cheng said to Sima Jian, "If the Zhou ruler does not listen, it will mean that your wisdom is trapped and your relations with Zhou are severed." "It would be better for you to say to the Zhou ruler: 'Who do you wish to appoint as heir?'" "Privately inform Jian that King Chu is now offering land resources to support him.'" "If you wish to become Crown Prince, have someone tell the Chancellor Yu Zhanzi and Qiangfu Kong that 'The king seems to want you to do this.'" "He is a capable man; his presence in the central government would be inconvenient for the Chancellor." The Chancellor then ordered him to become Crown Prince.


### Three Kingdoms Blockade of Qin

During the Three Kingdoms period, Ai Qin was appointed as a minister by Zhou; because he considered Qin to be light-minded, he delayed his departure. Someone said to the Prime Minister: "Whether Qin is light-minded or serious cannot yet be known." Qin wants to learn about the situation in the Three Kingdoms. You had better go directly to King Qin and say, 'Please advise His Majesty to listen to what is happening in the east.' Then King Qin will certainly value you highly. This is how you can gain importance for Zhou, by using Zhou to gain favor with Qin. Qi was valued because it had Zhou, and thus already gained Qin's support. "This is why Zhou has always maintained the friendship of a powerful state."


### Chang Ta Fled from the Western Zhou

Chang Ta sang the Western Zhou and went to the Eastern Zhou, conveying all the information about the Western Zhou to the Eastern Zhou. The Eastern Zhou was very pleased, while the Western Zhou was greatly angry. Feng Qie said, "I am able to kill him." The ruler gave him thirty jin of gold. Feng Qie sent someone to carry the gold and a letter, secretly delivering a message to Chang Ta that said: "Inform Chang Ta, the matter can be accomplished; strive to accomplish it; if it cannot be accomplished, flee away quickly." The matter will take too long and may be exposed; you would only bring about your own death." He then sent someone to inform the marquis of the Eastern Zhou, saying, "Tonight there will be a spy entering your territory." The marquis captured him and presented him to the Eastern Zhou; the Eastern Zhou immediately executed Chang Ta.


### Zhao Jian and Dong Zhou Become Hostile

Zhao Jian was at odds with Dong Zhou. Someone told Zhao Jian, "I will devise a secret plan for you." Zhao Jian said, "What is it?" "He said, 'Xizhou intensely hates Dongzhou. He once wanted to make Dongzhou and Chu hostile toward each other. Xizhou will certainly send assassins to attack you, pretending they are from Dongzhou, in order to gain favor with the king through this action.'" Zhao Jian said, "Good." "I am also afraid that Dongzhou might send assassins against me and thus provoke Chu's enmity toward Xizhou." He quickly reconciled with Dong Zhou.


### Yan Shi Was a Bandit

Yan Shi was a bandit, and Yang Jian participated in it. Dao Zhou, Duke Zhou kept him for fourteen days, sent him off in a carriage drawn by four horses. Han sent someone to reprimand Zhou, and the ruler of Zhou was worried about it. A guest said to the ruler of Zhou, "Speak plainly and say: 'I know that Yan Shi was a bandit and Yang Jian participated with him, so I kept him for fourteen days awaiting orders.'" "A small state is not sufficient to also harbor a bandit. Moreover, your envoy did not arrive, so I sent him away."
