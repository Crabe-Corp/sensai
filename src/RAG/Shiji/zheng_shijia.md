# Zheng Shijia

## 1

Zheng Huan Gong You was a younger son of King Li of Zhou and an illegitimate younger brother of King Xuan. In the twenty-second year of King Xuan's reign, You was first granted a fief in Zheng. At thirty-three years old when he was enfeoffed, the common people all found him convenient and loved him. King You appointed him as Minister of War. He united the people of Zhou, and all the people of Zhou were pleased; between He and Luo rivers, people found him convenient and thought of him. After one year as Minister of War, King You, because of the Bao concubine, had many corrupt policies in the royal court; some feudal lords rebelled against him. At this time, Gong Huan asked Taishi Bo, "The royal house has many troubles; where can I escape to avoid death?" Taishi Bo replied, "Only the eastern land east of Luo and south of He and Ji rivers is suitable for settlement." Gong asked, "Why so?" He replied, "The land is near Guo and Hui; the rulers of Guo and Hui are greedy and fond of profit, so the common people do not support them. Now that you are Minister of War, the people all love you. If you sincerely request to reside there, the rulers of Guo and HUI will see that you hold power and easily divide land for you." "If you truly settle there, the people of Guo and Hui will all become your people." Gong said, "I want to go south to the banks of the Jiang River. How is that?" He replied, "In the past, Zhurong was the fire official of Gaoshen Shi; his achievements were great, yet none from his lineage have risen in Zhou. Chu is his descendant. "When Zhou declines, Chu will surely rise." "If it rises, that would not be beneficial to Zheng." Gong said, "I want to settle in the west. How is that?" He replied, "Their people are greedy and profit-oriented; it would be difficult to live there for long." Gong said, "If Zhou declines, which state will rise?" He replied, "Perhaps Qi, Qin, Jin, and Chu? Qi is of the Jiang surname, descendants of Boyi. Boyi assisted Yao in establishing rituals. Qin is of the Ying surname, descendants of Boyi. Boyi assisted Shun in pacifying all things with gentleness. And the ancestors of Chu have also once contributed to the world. After King Wu of Zhou defeated ZHOU, King Cheng enfeoffed Shuyu in Tang. Its territory is perilous and difficult to access; therefore, with virtue and as Zhou declines, it will surely rise as well." Gong Huan said, "Good." Thus he finally addressed the king and moved his people east of Luo. Guo and Hui indeed contributed ten fiefs, which eventually became a state.

## 2

Two years later, the Quanrong killed King You at the foot of Lishan Mountain and also killed Gong Huan. The people of Zheng jointly enthroned his son Juetu, who became King Wu.

## 3

In the tenth year of King Wu's reign, he married a daughter of Shen Hou as his wife, named Wu Jiang. She gave birth to Crown Prince Wusheng, who was born in a difficult delivery; after he was born, the lady did not love him. Later she bore a younger son named Shuduan. Duan was easy to give birth to, and the lady loved him. In the twenty-seventh year, King Wu fell ill. The lady requested the king to appoint Duan as crown prince, but the king did not agree. In that year, King Wu died and Wusheng was enthroned; he became King Zhuang.

## 4

In the first year of King Zhuang's reign, his younger brother Duan was enfeoffed in Jing and given the title Taishu. Jizhong said, "Jing is larger than the state itself; this is not appropriate for enfeoffing an illegitimate son." King Zhuang said, "Lady Wu Jiang desires it; I dare not take it away from her." Duan arrived in Jing, repaired and prepared armor and weapons, and plotted with his mother Wu Jiang to attack Zheng. In the twenty-second year, Duan indeed launched an attack on Zheng, with Wu Jiang acting as an inside supporter. King Zhuang raised troops to attack Duan, and Duan fled. They attacked Jing; the people of Jing rebelled against Duan, who then fled to Yan. Yan fell into chaos, and Duan fled to Gong. At this time, King Zhuang moved his mother Wu Jiang to Chengying and made an oath: "Until we reach the Yellow Spring, we shall not see each other again." After more than a year, he regretted it and missed his mother. Kao Shou of Yinggu made an offering to the king, who rewarded him with food. Kao Shou said, "I have a mother; please allow me to offer this royal food to my mother." King Zhuang said, "I miss my mother very much and feel guilty about breaking the oath. What can I do?" Kao Shou said, "Dig a tunnel until you reach the Yellow Spring; then you will be able to meet." Thus he followed his advice and met his mother.

## 5

In the twenty-fourth year, Song Miu Gong died, and Gongzi Feng fled to Zheng. Zheng invaded Zhou territory and took its grain. In the twenty-fifth year, You of Zhou killed his king Huangong and declared himself ruler. He allied with Song to attack Zheng because of Gongzi Feng's presence there. In the twenty-seventh year, he first paid homage to King Huan of Zhou. King Huan was angry about Zheng's taking grain and did not treat him with courtesy. In the twenty-ninth year, King Zhuang, angered by Zhou's lack of courtesy, exchanged Bening and Xutan with Lu. In the thirty-third year, Song killed Kongfu. In the thirty-seventh year, King Zhuang did not pay homage to Zhou; King Huan of Zhou led Chen, Cai, Guo, and Wei in an attack on Zheng. King Zhuang, together with Jizhong and Gao Qumi, raised troops to defend himself, and the royal forces suffered a major defeat. Zhuchai shot and hit King Huan's arm. Zhuchai requested to follow up, but Duke Zheng stopped him, saying, "It is difficult enough to defy a superior and an elder; how could one dare to insult the Son of Heaven?" Thus he ceased. At night, he ordered Jizhong to inquire about the king's condition.

## 6

In the thirty-eighth year, Beirong attacked Qi; Qi sent envoys to request help, and Zheng dispatched Crown Prince Hu with troops to rescue Qi. Qi Ligong wanted to give his daughter in marriage to him, but Hu declined, saying, "I am a small state and not the equal of Qi." At that time, Jizhong was with him and advised him to accept the marriage, saying, "The king has many favored concubines; if the crown prince lacks strong support, he will not be enthroned. The three younger princes are all potential kings." The so-called three young princes were Crown Prince Hu, his younger brother Tu, and the next younger brother Ziwei.

## 7

In the forty-third year, King Zhuang of Zheng died. Initially, Jizhong had great favor with King Zhuang; King Zhuang appointed him as an official; the king arranged for him to marry a woman from Deng, and they had Crown Prince Hu. Therefore, Jizhong enthroned him; he became King Zhao.

## 8

King Zhuang also married a woman from Song Yong Shi and had King Li Tu. The woman of Song Yong Shi was favored in Song. Song Zhuang Gong, upon hearing that Jizhong had enthroned Hu, sent people to lure and arrest Jizhong, saying, "If you do not place Tu on the throne, you will die." He also detained Tu in exchange for a bribe. Jizhong agreed with Song and formed an alliance with them. He returned Tu to Zheng and enthroned him. Zhao Gong Hu, upon hearing that Jizheng had used Song to demand the enthronement of his younger brother Tu, on the Dinghai day of the ninth month (September 15, 720 BC), Hu fled to Wei. On the Ji Hai day, Tu arrived in Zheng and was enthroned; he became known as King Li.

## 9

In the fourth year of King Li's reign, Jizhong monopolized state affairs. King Li was troubled by this and secretly ordered his son-in-law Yong Jiu to kill Jizhong. Jiu's wife, who was the daughter of Jizhong, learned about this and asked her mother, "Between father and husband, which is closer?" Her mother said, "A father is one and irreplaceable; a husband can be replaced by anyone." The daughter then informed Jizhong of the plot, and Jizhong turned around and killed Yong Jiu, executing him in the marketplace. King Li had no way to deal with Jizhong and was angry at Jiu, saying, "To plot with a woman—death is indeed deserved!" In summer, King Li left the capital and took residence in Lili, an outlying town. Jizhong welcomed Zhao Gong Hu; on the Yihai day of the sixth month, he re-entered Zheng and was enthroned.

## 10

In autumn, King Li Tu took advantage of the people in Lili to kill his official Shanbo and then settled there. The feudal lords heard that King Li had fled, attacked Zheng, but failed and left. Song provided a considerable amount of troops to King Li, who defended himself in Lili; Zheng did not attack Lili because of this.

## 11

In the second year of King Zhao's reign, since Crown Prince Hu was still a crown prince, his father King Zhuang wanted to appoint Gao Qumi as an official. Crown Prince Hu disliked this, but King Zhuang did not listen and eventually appointed Qumi as an official. When King Zhao was enthroned, he feared that Qumi would kill him. On the Xinmao day of the tenth month in winter, while hunting with Gao Qumi and King Zhao, Qumi shot and killed King Zhao in the field. Jizhong and Qumi dared not bring King Li back, so they instead enthroned Ziwei, the younger brother of King Zhao, as ruler. He became known as Ziwei; no posthumous title was given to him.

## 12

In the first year of Ziwei's reign, in July, Qi Xianggong held a meeting with feudal lords at Shouzhi. Ziwei went to attend it, accompanied by Gao Qumi as his assistant; Jizhong claimed illness and did not go along. The reason for this was that Ziwei had once fought with Qi Xianggong when the latter was still a prince, and they were enemies. When meeting with feudal lords, Jizhong requested that Ziwei not attend. Ziwei said, "Qi is powerful, and King Li resides in Lili. If I do not go, it would mean leading the feudal lords to attack me and support King Li." "I had better go; why should going necessarily bring disgrace? And how could it come to this!" He finally went. At that time, Jizheng feared that Qi would kill Ziwei as well, so he claimed illness. When Ziwei arrived, he did not apologize to the Duke of Qi; the Duke was angry and then ambushed and killed Ziwei. Gao Qumi fled back, returned, and plotted with Jizhong; they summoned Gongzi Ying, the younger brother of Ziwei, from Chen and enthroned him, who became known as Zhengzi. In this year, Qi Xianggong sent Pengsheng to kill Lu Huangong by dragging him while drunk.

## 13

In the eighth year of Zhengzi's reign, Qi people Guan Zhifu and others rebelled, assassinating their ruler Xianggong. In the twelfth year [of Zhengzi's reign], a man named Zhangwan from Song assassinated his ruler Min Gong. Jizhong of Zheng died.

## 14

In the fourteenth year, former King Li of Zheng, Tu, who was in Lili, sent people to entice and kidnap Zheng official Fu Jia, demanding that he be allowed back into power. Fu Jia said, "Let me go, and I will kill Zhengzi for you so that you may return to power." King Li made an oath with him and then released Fu Jia. On the Jiayou day of the sixth month, Fu Jia killed Zhengzi and his two sons and welcomed back King Li Tu, who returned from Lili to reclaim the throne. Originally, a snake inside and a snake outside fought in the middle of Zheng's southern gate; the inner snake died. After six years, King Li indeed returned to power. Upon his return, he reproached his paternal uncle Yuan, saying, "I was exiled from my country and lived abroad. You had no intention of bringing me back; this is indeed excessive." Yuan said, "Loyalty to one's ruler without divided loyalty is the duty of a subject. Uncle Yuan knows he has committed a crime." He then committed suicide. King Li then said to Fu Jia, "You have shown divided loyalty toward your ruler." He then executed him. Fu Jia said, "To receive great kindness yet not repay it—truly so!"

## 15

In the first year after King Li Tu's reign began, Duke Huan of Qi started to establish his hegemony.

## 16

In the fifth year, Yan and Wei, together with Tuai, the younger brother of King Hui of Zhou, attacked the king. The king fled to Wen and enthroned his younger brother Tuai as ruler. In the sixth year, King Hui of Zhou sent an urgent request for help to Zheng. King Li mobilized troops to attack Crown Prince Tuai of Zhou but failed to defeat him. As a result, he returned with King Hui; the king resided in Lili. In spring of the seventh year, King Li of Zheng and Gou Shu launched a surprise attack to kill Crown Prince Tuai and helped King Hui return to Zhou.

## 17

In autumn, King Li died; his son Wen Gong Jie was enthroned. King Li had been on the throne for four years at first, then fled and lived in Lili. He remained there for seventeen years before returning to power, ruling for another seven years; altogether, his time away from power totaled twenty-eight years.

## 18

In the twenty-fourth year, a low-ranking concubine of Wen Gong named Yan Ji dreamed that Heaven gave her an orchid and said, "I am Boyao. I am your ancestor." Take this as your son; the orchid possesses a fragrance that is renowned in the land." She told Wen Gong about her dream, and Wen Gong favored her, giving her an orchid plant as a token. Soon after, she gave birth to a son, whom they named Lan.

## 19

In the thirty-sixth year, Jin Gongzi Chonger passed through Zheng; Wen Gong did not treat him with courtesy. Wen Gong's younger brother, Shu Zhan, said, "Chonger is virtuous and also of the same clan. He has come to you in hardship; it would be inappropriate to show him no courtesy." Wen Gong said, "Many exiled sons of feudal lords have passed through here; how could I possibly treat them all with courtesy!" Shu Zhan said, "If you do not show him courtesy, then kill him; if you do not kill him, allowing him to return to his state will bring trouble for Zheng." Wen Gong did not listen.

## 20

In spring of the thirty-seventh year, Jin Gongzi Chonge returned to his state and was enthroned; he became known as Duke Wen. In autumn, Zheng attacked Hua, which submitted to Zheng's command. Soon after, however, Hua defected and allied with Wei; thus, Zheng launched another attack on Hua. King Xiang of Zhou sent Bo Fu to request the return of Hua. Zheng Wen Gong resented the fact that King Hui had been exiled to Lili, and his father, King Li, had helped him return. Yet King Hui did not bestow a title or rewards upon King Li. He also resented King Xiang for supporting Wei and Hua; therefore, he refused King Xiang's request and imprisoned Bo Fu. The king was angry and allied with the Di people to attack Zheng, but failed to conquer them. In winter, the Di attacked King Xiang; King Xiang fled and took refuge in Zheng, where Wen Gong of Zheng housed him at Fan. In the thirty-eighth year, Duke Wen of Jin helped King Xiang return to Chengzhou.

## 21

In the forty-first year, Zheng assisted Chu in attacking Jin. Because of the lack of courtesy shown to Duke Wen of Jin when he passed through, Zheng turned its back on Jin and supported Chu instead. In the forty-third year, Duke Wen of Jin and Duke Mu of Qin jointly besieged Zheng to punish it for aiding Chu in attacking Jin, as well as for the lack of courtesy shown when Duke Wen passed through. Originally, Wen Gong of Zheng had three principal consorts and five favored sons; all died young due to crimes. The ruler was angry and expelled the group of rival princes. Zi Lan fled to Jin, where he joined Duke Wen in besieging Zheng. At that time, Zi Lan served Duke Wen of Jin with great diligence and was favored by him. He secretly approached Jin to request being installed as the crown prince in Zheng. Jin therefore wanted to capture Shu Zhan for punishment. Wen Gong of Zheng was afraid and dared not speak to Shu Zhan about it. Shu Zhan heard about this and said to the ruler of Zheng, "I advised you earlier, but you did not listen to me. Jin will indeed become a problem. However, the reason why Jin is besieging Zheng is because of me, Shu Zhan. If I die and Zheng is spared, it will fulfill my wish." He then committed suicide. The people of Zheng delivered Shu Zhan's corpse to Jin. Duke Wen of Jin said, "I must see the ruler of Zheng once and humiliate him before leaving." The people of Zheng were worried about this, so they sent someone secretly to Qin and said, "Destroying Zheng would only benefit Jin; it is not in Qin's interest." The Qin army withdrew. Duke Wen of Jin wanted to install Lan as the crown prince and informed Zheng about it. Zheng official Shi Gui said, "I have heard that Ji surname was the principal wife of Houji; descendants from her line should rise to prominence. Zi Lan's mother is a descendant of them." Moreover, all the sons born to the principal consorts have already died; among the other sons, none are as virtuous as Lan. Now that the siege is urgent and Jin has made a request, what greater benefit could there be!" Thus, they agreed to Jin's request, formed an alliance, and finally enthroned Zi Lan as crown prince; the Jin army then withdrew.

## 22

In the forty-fifth year, Wen Gong died; his son Lan was enthroned, who became known as Miao Gong.

## 23

In the first year of Miao Gong's reign, in spring, Duke Mu of Qin sent three generals with troops to launch a surprise attack on Zheng. When they reached Hua, they encountered Zheng merchant Xian Gao, who pretended to offer twelve oxen as a gift to welcome their army. Thus, the Qin forces did not proceed further and returned; however, Jin defeated them at Xiao Pass. Originally, the previous year, when Wen Gong of Zheng died, Cheng Zeng, an official in charge of city affairs in Zheng, betrayed Zheng's situation to Qin; that was why the Qin army came. In the third year, Zheng mobilized troops and joined Jin in attacking Qin, defeating the Qin forces at Wang.

## 24

The previous year, Crown Prince Shangchen of Chu assassinated his father King Chengwang and took over the throne in his place. In the twenty-first year, he joined Song's Hua Yuan in attacking Zheng. Hua Yuan slaughtered sheep to feed the soldiers but did not give any to his charioteer Yang Zhen, who became angry and drove off toward Zheng; Zheng captured Hua Yuan. Song ransomed Hua Yuan, but Yuan fled away as well. Jin sent Zhao Chuan with troops to attack Zheng.

## 25

In spring of the first year of Ling Gong's reign, Chu presented a giant soft-shelled turtle as a tribute to King Ling. Zijia and Zigong were about to pay homage to Ling Gong. Zigong's index finger twitched, so he said to Zijia, "On other occasions when my finger twitches, I must be about to eat something unusual." When they entered and saw King Ling serving turtle soup, Zigong laughed and said, "Indeed it is!" King Ling asked why he was laughing, so Zigong told him the whole story. King Ling summoned them and gave the soup to everyone except Zigong. Zigong was angry, dipped his finger into the soup, tasted it, and then left. King Ling became furious and wanted to kill Zigong. Zigong plotted with Zijia first. In summer, they assassinated King Ling. The people of Zheng wanted to enthroned Qu Ji, the younger brother of King Ling. Qu Ji declined and said, "If we must choose based on virtue, then I am not virtuous; if we must follow seniority, then Gongzi Jian is the eldest." Jian was a younger brother of King Ling and an older brother to Qu Ji. Thus, they enthroned Gongzi Jian, who became known as Xiang Gong.

## 26

After Xiang Gong was enthroned, he planned to completely remove the Miao clan. The Miao family were relatives of those who had killed King Ling and Zigong. Qu Ji said, "If you must remove the Miao clan, I will be the one to do it." Thus, they stopped. They were all appointed as officials of high rank.

## 27

In the first year of Xiang Gong's reign, Chu was angry that Zheng had accepted a bribe from Song and released Hua Yuan, so they launched an attack on Zheng. Zheng defected from Chu and became close to Jin. In the fifth year, Chu again attacked Zheng, and Jin came to rescue them. In the sixth year, Zijia died, and the people of the state again drove out his clan because they had assassinated King Ling.

## 28

In the seventh year, Zheng formed an alliance with Jin at Yanling. In the eighth year, King Zhuang of Chu, because Zheng had allied with Jin, launched an attack. He besieged Zheng for three months, and Zheng surrendered to Chu by opening its gates. King Chu entered through the Huang Gate, and Zheng Xiang Gong met him bare-chested with a sheep in hand, saying: "I was unable to govern my outlying territories properly, causing Your Majesty's anger to reach this humble city. This is my fault. How dare I not obey your command?" If Your Majesty wishes to relocate me to the southern Jiang region, or to bestow me upon other feudal lords, I shall also obey your command without hesitation. If Your Majesty does not forget King Li and King Xuan, or Gong Huan and Gong Wu, and out of compassion cannot bear to sever their ancestral state, and instead bestows a barren land upon me so that I may once again serve Your Majesty, this would be my greatest wish, though it is something I dare not hope for. I now humbly lay bare my heart and soul; I am ready to obey your command in all things." King Zhuang then withdrew thirty li before halting. The ministers of Chu said, "From Ying to this place, the officials and soldiers have been laboring for a long time. Now that we have captured the state but are about to abandon it, what do you think?" King Zhuang said, "The reason for our attack was to punish those who did not submit. Now that they have submitted, what more could we possibly seek?" In the end, he withdrew. Jin heard of Chu's attack on Zheng and sent troops to rescue Zheng. However, Jin hesitated between two courses of action, so they were delayed; by the time they reached the Yellow River, the Chu forces had already withdrawn. The Jin generals debated whether to cross the river or return, but in the end decided to cross it. King Zhuang heard of this and returned to attack Jin. Zheng, in turn, assisted Chu, and the two forces decisively defeated the Jin army by the river. In the tenth year, Jin launched an attack on Zheng because Zheng had defected from Jin and allied with Chu.

## 29

In the eleventh year, King Zhuang of chu attacked Song, and Song urgently appealed to Jin for help. Jin Jinggong wanted to send troops to rescue Song, but Bo Zong advised the ruler of Jin, saying: "Heaven is currently favoring Chu; it is not yet time to attack them." They then sought out a brave warrior named Huo Ren Jie Yang, courtesy name Zihu, and sent him to deceive Chu, ordering Song not to surrender. While passing through Zheng, which was allied with Chu, they captured Jie Yang and delivered him to Chu. King Chu offered generous rewards and made agreements, urging Jie Yang to reverse his words and order Song to surrender quickly. After three demands, he finally agreed. Thus, Chu placed Jie Yang on a high cart and ordered him to call out to Song. Yet, he defied the agreement with Chu and conveyed his ruler's command from Jin, saying: "Jin is mobilizing its entire army to rescue Song. Although Song may be in dire straits, be cautious not to surrender to Chu; the Jin forces are coming now!" King Zhuang of Chu was greatly enraged and intended to kill him. Jie Yang said, "A ruler who can issue commands in accordance with righteousness is virtuous; a minister who can carry out those commands is trustworthy. I received my lord's command to go forth, and I would rather die than fail in it." King Zhuang said: "You promised me, yet you have betrayed that promise. Where is your trustworthiness?" Jie Yang replied, "I agreed to Your Majesty's request in order to fulfill my lord's command." As he was about to die, Jie Yang turned to the Chu army and said: "As a subject, never forget that complete loyalty leads to a noble death!" King Zhuang's younger brothers all advised the king to pardon him, and thus King Zhuang pardoned Jie Yang and sent him back. Jin bestowed upon him the title of Shangqing (a high-ranking official).

## 30

In the eighteenth year, Xiang Gong died and was succeeded by his son Dougong Fei.

## 31

In the first year of Dougong's reign, Gong Yan offended Zheng at Chu; in response, Dougong sent his younger brother Hui to Chu to plead their case. As the plea was unfounded, Chu imprisoned Hui. Thus, Zheng Dougong came to make peace with Jin and became their ally once more. Hui privately approached the ruler of Chu, Zifan, who then spoke on his behalf and arranged for Hui's return to Zheng.

## 32

In the second year, Chu attacked Zheng, and Jin forces came to rescue them. That same year, Dougong died, and his younger brother Hui was enthroned; he became known as Chenggong.

## 33

In the third year of Chenggong's reign, King Gong of Chu said, "Zheng Chenggong is a virtuous ruler," and sent envoys to form an alliance with him. Chenggong secretly formed the alliance. In autumn, Chenggong paid a visit to Jin, but Jin accused Zheng of having privately made peace with Chu and detained him. They sent Luan Shu to attack Zheng. In the spring of the fourth year, as Zheng was troubled by Jin's siege, Gongzi Ru installed Chenggong's illegitimate older brother Xu as ruler. In the fourth month of that year, when Jin learned that Zheng had enthroned a new ruler, they returned Chenggong to him. The people of Zheng heard that Chenggong was returning and killed their current ruler Xu, welcoming Chenggong back instead. Jin's troops withdrew.

## 34

In the tenth year, Zheng broke its alliance with Jin and formed an alliance with Chu instead. King Li of Jin was furious and launched a military campaign against Zheng. King Gong of Chu came to the rescue of Zheng. The Jin and Chu forces clashed at Yanling, where the Chu army was defeated. Jin wounded King Gong of Chu in the eye, after which both sides withdrew from the battlefield. In the thirteenth year, Duke Dao of Jin attacked Zheng and stationed his troops at Weishang. Zheng defended its city walls, so Jin withdrew as well.

## 35

In the fourteenth year, Chenggong died and was succeeded by his son Yun. He became known as Liggong.

## 36

In the fifth year of Ligong's reign, Zheng's prime minister Zisi paid homage to Ligong, but Ligong treated him with disrespect. Zisi was furious and ordered the cooks to poison Ligong, then informed the feudal lords that "Ligong died suddenly of an illness." They enthroned Ligong's son Jia. At that time, Jia was only five years old; he became known as Jianguanggong.

## 37

In the first year of Jianguanggong's reign, several members of the royal family plotted to kill the prime minister Zisi. However, Zisi discovered their plot and instead had all the princes executed. In the second year, Jin attacked Zheng; Zheng formed an alliance with them, so Jin withdrew. In winter, Zheng once again formed an alliance with Chu. Zisi feared punishment, so he maintained close relations with both Jin and Chu. In the third year, Zisi plotted to declare himself ruler, but Gongzi Kong sent Wei Zhi to kill Zisi and took his place instead. Zikong also wanted to declare himself as sovereign. Zichan said, "Zisi acted inappropriately and was punished for it; now you are following his example. If this continues, there will be no end to the chaos." Thus, Zikong heeded his advice and served as prime minister under Zheng Jian Gong.

## 38

In the fourth year, Jin was angry that Zheng had formed an alliance with Chu and attacked Zheng; Zheng then formed an alliance with Jin. King Gong of Chu came to rescue Zheng and defeated the Jin forces. Jianguanggong wished to make peace with Jin, but Chu detained Zheng's envoys once again.

## 39

In the twelfth year, Jianguanggong was angry at Zikong's monopolization of state power and had him executed, appointing Zichan as an official instead. In the nineteenth year, Jianguanggong went to Jin to request that Weigong return and granted Zichan six fiefs as a reward. Zichan declined the offer but eventually accepted three of the fiefs. In the twenty-second year, Wu sent Yanning Jisi to Zheng. He met Zichan and treated him as an old friend, saying to Zichan: "The current rulers of Zheng are extravagant; difficulties will soon come, and you will likely be involved in governance. If you take charge of the government, you must govern according to propriety; otherwise, Zheng will fall." Zichan treated Jisi with great hospitality. In the twenty-third year, members of the royal family vied for favor and killed each other; they also plotted to kill Zichan. One prince advised, "Zichan is a kind and virtuous man; Zheng has survived because of Zichan. Do not kill him!" Thus, they stopped.

## 40

In the twenty-fifth year, Zheng sent Zichan to Jin to inquire about Duke Ping's illness. Duke Ping said: "The diviners say that Shichen and Taidai are causing the illness, but the historians do not know what this means. I dare to ask you?" Zichan replied: "The family of Gaoshen had two sons, the elder named Ebo and the younger Shichen. They lived in a vast forest but were unable to get along, fighting with weapons every day. Later emperors disapproved of this and relocated Ebo to Shangqiu, where he presided over the constellation Shen. The people of Shang inherited his legacy, hence the constellation is known as the Shang Star." Shichen was relocated to Daya, where he presided over Shen. The Tang people inherited this tradition and served the Xia and Shang dynasties; in its later period, it was known as Tang Shuyu. When King Wu's wife Yijiang was pregnant with Marquis Dushu, she dreamed that the emperor told her: "I have named your son Yu; I will give him Tang and entrust it to Shen so that his descendants may flourish." When he was born, there was a mark on his palm reading "Yu," so they named him accordingly. When King Cheng destroyed the state of Tang, he established it as the fief of Marquis Dushu. Therefore, Shen is the star of Jin. lington From this we can see that Shichen is the deity of Shen. In ancient times, Jin Tian Shi had a descendant named Mi, who served as the teacher of Xuanming; he fathered Yunge and Taidai. Taidai was able to carry on his official duties, managing the Fen and Tao rivers, blocking large lakes, and settling in Taiyuan. The emperor praised him for this and granted him the region of Fenchuan as his fief. The states of Shen, Si, Ru, and Huang actually maintained its ancestral rites. Now the state of Jin controls Fenchuan but has destroyed it. From this we can see, Taidai is the deity of Fen and Tao rivers. However, these two deities do not harm your person. The gods of mountains and rivers are responsible for warding off calamities such as floods and droughts; the deities of the sun, moon, stars, and constellations are responsible for preventing untimely snow, frost, wind, and rain; if your illness is due to improper diet, sorrow or joy, or indulgence in women." Duke Ping and Shuxiang said: "Excellent! A truly erudite gentleman!" They offered Zichan generous gifts in return.

## 41

In the summer of the twenty-seventh year, Duke Jian of Zheng paid a court visit to Jin. In winter, fearing the strength of King Ling of Chu, he again paid homage to Chu, with Zichan accompanying him. In the twenty-eighth year, Duke Zheng fell ill and sent Zichan to meet with the feudal lords. They concluded an alliance with King Ling of Chu in Shen and executed Qing Feng of Qi.

## 42

In the thirty-sixth year, Duke Jian died, and his son Ning was enthroned as Duke Ding. In autumn, Duke Ding paid a court visit to Duke Zhao of Jin.

## 43

In the first year of Duke Ding's reign, Gongzi Qiji assassinated his king, King Ling, and declared himself ruler as King Ping. He wished to demonstrate virtue among the feudal lords. He returned the territories of Zheng that had been seized by King Ling back to Zheng.

## 44

In the fourth year, Duke Zhao of Jin died; his six high-ranking ministers were powerful while the royal house was weak. Zichan said to Han Xunzi: "To govern must be done with virtue; do not forget the reason you were placed in power." In the sixth year, a fire broke out in Zheng, and the duke wanted to perform rituals to ward it off. Zichan said: "It is better to cultivate virtue."

## 45

In the eighth year, Crown Prince Jian of Chu fled to Zheng. In the tenth year, Crown Prince Jian conspired with Jin to attack Zheng. Zheng killed Jian, and Jian's son Sheng fled to Wu.

## 46

In the eleventh year, Duke Ding went to Jin. Jin and Zheng plotted together to punish Zhou's rebellious ministers and installed King Jing in the Zhou capital.

## 47

In the thirteenth year, Duke Ding died, and his son Chai was enthroned as Duke Xian. Duke Xian died in the thirteenth year of his reign; his son Shenggong Sheng succeeded him. At that time, Jin's six high-ranking ministers were strong and encroached upon Zheng; thus, Zheng became weak.

## 48

In the fifth year of Duke Sheng's reign, Zichan, the prime minister of Zheng, died; all the people of Zheng wept and mourned him as if they had lost a close relative. Zichan was the youngest son of Duke Cheng of Zheng. He was a kind and compassionate person, loyal and sincere in serving his ruler. Confucius once passed through Zheng and treated Zichan like a brother. When he heard of Zichan's death, Confucius wept and said: "He was the last remnant of virtue from ancient times!"

## 49

In the eighth year, the Fan and Zhonghang clans rebelled against Jin and sent an urgent plea for help to Zheng; Zheng came to their aid. Jin attacked Zheng and defeated its army at Tie.

## 50

In the fourteenth year, Duke Jing of Song destroyed the state of Cao. In the twentieth year, Tian Chang assassinated his ruler, Duke Jian of Qi, and became prime minister of Qi. In the twenty-second year, King Hui of Chu destroyed the state of Chen. Confucius died.

## 51

In the thirty-sixth year, Zhi Bo of Jin attacked Zheng and captured nine towns.

## 52

In the thirty-seventh year, Duke Sheng died, and his son Yi was enthroned as Duke Ai. In the eighth year of Duke Ai's reign, the people of Zheng assassinated him and enthroned Shenggong's younger brother Chou; he became known as Duke Gong. In the third year of Duke Gong's reign, the three Jin states destroyed Zhi Bo. In the thirty-first year, Duke Gong died, and his son Yigong Yi was enthroned. In the first year of Yigong's reign, Han Wuzi attacked Zheng and killed him. The people of Zheng enthroned Yigong's younger brother Taidai; he became known as Xugong.

## 53

In the fifteenth year of Xugong's reign, Duke Jinghou of Han attacked Zheng and captured Yongqiu. Zheng built a city at Jing.

## 54

In the sixteenth year, Zheng attacked Han and defeated its forces at Fusu. In the twentieth year, Han, Zhao, and Wei were officially recognized as feudal lords. In the twenty-third year, Zheng besieged Yangzhi of Han.

## 55

In the twenty-fifth year, the ruler of Zheng killed his prime minister Ziyang. In the twenty-seventh year, supporters of Ziyang jointly assassinated Xugong Taidai and enthroned Yigong's younger brother Yi as ruler; he became known as King Zheng. King Zheng Yi reigned for two years, when Fushu of Zheng rebelled and returned to Han. In the eleventh year, Han attacked Zheng and captured Yangcheng.

## 56

In the twenty-first year, Duke Ai of Han destroyed Zheng and annexed its territory.

## 57

Grand Historian said: There is a saying, "Those who associate for the sake of power and profit will become estranged once such advantages vanish"—Fu Xia was an example of this. Although Fu Xia had forced the murder of Duke Zi and installed King Li, King Li eventually betrayed him and had him killed. What difference is there between this and Li Ke of Jin? Xun Xi maintained his principles; he died but could not preserve the rule of Xiqi. The changes that have taken place, and their origins, are indeed numerous!
