# Table of Years of Famous Ministers and Generals since the Rise of the Han

## 1

On the jiazi day of the first month (February 13, 420 AD), Emperor Gong of Jin abdicated. On the yiji day of the same month (March 7, 420 AD) Liu Yu was enthroned as emperor and changed the era name to Jianyuan. The state's title was changed from Jin to Song.
