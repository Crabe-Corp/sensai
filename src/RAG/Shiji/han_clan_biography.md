# Han Clan Biography

## 1

Han Zhixian was of the same surname as the Zhou dynasty, surnamed Ji Shi. Later descendants served the Jin state and were granted a fief at Hanyuan, known as Han Wuzi. Three generations after Wuzi there was Han Jue, who adopted the surname of his fief and became a member of the Han clan.

## 2

Han Jue, in the third year of King Jinggong's reign of Jin, the Jin State Minister of Justice Tu Anjia was about to instigate a rebellion and plotted to kill Zhao Dun, who had been an assassin of Duke Linggong. Zhao Dun had already died, so they wanted to execute his son Zhao Shuo. Han Jue tried to stop Tu Anjia, but Tu did not listen. Jue informed Zhao Shuo and ordered him to flee. Shuo said, "If you are sure that the ancestral rites of the Zhao clan will not be extinguished, I would have no regrets even if I die." Han Jue agreed. When Tu Anjia exterminated the Zhao clan, Han Jue claimed illness and did not go out. Cheng Ying and Gongsun Chuju had hidden Zhao Wu, the orphan of the Zhao clan; Han Jue knew about this.

## 3

In the eleventh year of King Jinggong, Han Jue and Xi Ke led an army of eight hundred chariots to attack Qi. They defeated King Qinggong of Qi at An and captured Peng Choufu. At that time, Jin established six high-ranking officials known as the Six Ministers, and Han Jue held one of these positions, being titled Xianzi.

## 4

In the seventeenth year of King Jinggong's reign, he fell ill and diviners determined that an unfinished great undertaking was causing the illness. Han Jue cited the merits of Zhao Chengji, noting that he now had no descendants to perform ancestral rites, in order to move King Jinggong. King Jinggong asked, "Are there still any descendants left?" Han Jue then spoke of Zhao Wu and thus restored the former lands and fiefs to the old Zhao clan, continuing their ancestral rites.

## 5

In the seventh year of King Daogong's reign (tenth year was corrected), Han Xianzi grew old. After Xianzi died, his son Xuanzi succeeded him. Xuanzi moved to live in Zhou.

## 6

In the fourteenth year of King Pinggong's reign, Wu Jizha was sent as an envoy to Jin and said, "The governance of the Jin state will ultimately belong to Han, Wei, and Zhao." In the twelfth year of King Qinggong's reign, Han Xuanzi divided ten counties of the Qi and Yangshe clans together with Zhao and Wei. In the fifteenth year of King Dinggong's reign, Xuanzi and Zhao Jianzi launched an invasion against the Fan and Zhonghang clans. After Xuanzi died, his son Zhenzi succeeded him as leader. Zhenzi moved to live in Pingyang.

## 7

After Zhenzi died, his son Jianzi succeeded him. After Jianzi died, his son Zhuangzi succeeded him. After Zhuangzi died, his son Kangzi succeeded him. Kangzi, together with Zhao Xiangzi and Wei Huanzi, defeated Zhī Bo, divided his territory, and their lands expanded greatly, surpassing those of the feudal lords.

## 8

After Kangzi died, his son Wuzi succeeded him. In the second year of Wuzi's reign, he attacked Zheng and killed its ruler Yougong. In the sixteenth year, Wuzi died, and his son Duke Jing was enthroned.

## 9

In the first year of Duke Jing Qian's reign, he attacked Zheng, capturing Yongqiu. In the second year, Zheng defeated our forces at Fushu.

## 10

In the sixth year, together with Zhao and Wei, they were all granted the status of feudal lords.

## 11

In the ninth year, Zheng besieged Yangzhi in our territory. After Duke Jing died, his son Duke Lie was enthroned as ruler.

## 12

In the third year of Duke Lie's reign, Nie Zheng killed Han's prime minister Xia Lai. In the ninth year, Qin attacked Yiyang in our territory and captured six towns. In the thirteenth year, Duke Lie died, and his son King Wen was enthroned. That same year, King Wen of Wei died.

## 13

In the second year of King Wen's reign, he attacked Zheng and captured Yangcheng. He attacked Song, reaching Pengcheng, where he captured its ruler. In the seventh year, he attacked Qi and reached Sangqiu. Zheng rebelled against Jin. In the ninth year, he attacked Qi again and reached Lingqiu. In the tenth year, King Wen died, and his son Duke Ai was enthroned.

## 14

In the first year of Duke Ai's reign, together with Zhao and Wei they divided the Jin state. In the second year, he destroyed Zheng and moved the capital to Zheng.

## 15

In the sixth year, Han Yan assassinated his ruler Duke Ai. His son Duke Yi was enthroned in his place.

## 16

In the second year of Duke Yi's reign, Wei defeated us at Maling. In the fifth year, he met with King Huaiwang of Wei in Zhaiyang. In the ninth year, Wei defeated us at Dian. In the twelfth year, Duke Yi died, and his son King Zhao was enthroned.

## 17

In the first year of King Zhao's reign, Qin defeated us at Xishan. In the second year, Song captured Huangchi in our territory. Wei took Zhu. In the sixth year, he attacked Dongzhou and captured Lingguan and Xingqiu.

## 18

In the eighth year, Shen Buhai became prime minister of Han. He refined his methods and practiced governance; as a result, the state was well governed, and no feudal lords came to invade.

## 19

In the tenth year, Han Ji assassinated his ruler King Dao. In the eleventh year, King Zhao went to Qin. In the twenty-second year, Shen Buhai died. In the twenty-fourth year, Qin captured Yiyang in our territory.

## 20

In the twenty-fifth year, there was a drought; they built Gaomen. Qu Yijiu said: "King Zhao will not leave through this gate." Why is that? Because it was not in accordance with the proper time. What I mean by "time" is not about days or hours; people naturally have times of fortune and misfortune. King Zhao once had a time of good fortune, yet he did not build Gaomen. Last year Qin captured Yiyang, and this year there was a drought. King Zhao did not use this time to relieve the people's distress but instead indulged in luxury; this is called 'acting with excess when resources are scarce.'" In the twenty-sixth year, Gaomen was completed. King Zhao died and indeed did not leave through this gate. His son, King Xuanhui, was enthroned.

## 21

In the fifth year of King Xuanhui's reign, Zhang Yi became prime minister of Qin. In the eighth year, Wei defeated our general Han Ju. In the eleventh year, the ruler assumed the title of king. He met with Zhao at Qu Shu. In the fourteenth year, Qin defeated us in Yan.

## 22

In the sixteenth year, Qin defeated our forces at Xiuyu and captured Han generals Geng and Shenchai in Zuzhe. The Han state was in dire straits. Gongzhong told King Han, "Alliance states cannot be relied upon." "Now, Qin has long desired to attack Chu. Your Majesty might as well take advantage of Zhang Yi to make peace with Qin, offering them a famous capital city as tribute, preparing armor and weapons, and joining forces with them to attack the south against Chu. This is a strategy of exchanging one for two." King Han said, "Good idea." He then ordered Gongzhong to prepare for the journey and head west to negotiate with Qin. King Chu heard of this and was greatly alarmed, summoning Chen Zhen to inform him. Chen Zhen said, "Qin has long desired to attack Chu; now they have also obtained one famous capital city from Han and are preparing armor. If Qin and Han join forces to attack Chu, this is exactly what Qin has prayed for." Now that they have already obtained it, Chu will certainly be attacked. "Your Majesty should listen to my advice and alert the entire realm. Mobilize troops, announce that we are rescuing Han, deploy war chariots along the roads, send envoys with many carriages and generous gifts, so as to make Qin believe that Your Majesty is coming to their aid." "Even if Han cannot heed our advice, they will surely be grateful to Your Majesty. They certainly won't act in unison with Qin against us; this would mean discord between Qin and Han. Even if the army arrives, Chu need not suffer greatly." "If we can persuade Han to break relations with Qin, Qin will certainly be furious and harbor deep resentment toward Han. "Han's southern alliance with Chu will surely weaken its relationship with Qin; "By weakening their ties to Qin, Han's response to Qin will lack respect. This would mean using the combined forces of Qin and Han to avoid a disaster for Chu." King Chu said, "Good idea." He then alerted the entire realm and mobilized troops, declaring that they would rescue Han. He ordered war chariots to fill the roads, dispatched envoys with numerous carriages and heavy gifts. They told King Han: "Although my state is small, I have already fully mobilized all available forces." "I hope your great state will proceed to fulfill its ambitions against Qin. I shall sacrifice Chu for the sake of Han." King Han heard this and was greatly pleased, so he stopped Gongzhong's journey to Qin. Gongzhong said, "No." "The one who truly attacks us is Qin; the one offering only empty words of aid is Chu. "Your Majesty relies on the hollow promise from Chu, yet lightly breaks ties with a powerful enemy like Qin; Your Majesty will surely be laughed at by all under heaven. "Moreover, the states of Chu and Han are not allied as brothers, nor have they long-standing agreements to plan an attack on Qin. "Now that there is already a show of force, and yet troops are sent with the claim of rescuing Han, this must be Chen Zhen's plan. "Moreover, Your Majesty has already sent envoys to inform Qin; if we do not proceed now, it will be an act of deception toward Qin. "To lightly deceive a powerful state like Qin and place trust in the schemes of Chu's ministers, I fear Your Majesty will regret it." King Han did not listen, so he severed relations with Qin. Qin was greatly enraged and increased its forces to attack Han; a major battle ensued, but the aid from Chu never arrived in Han. In the nineteenth year, they heavily defeated our forces at Anmen. Prince Cang was sent as a hostage to Qin in order to secure peace.

## 23

In the twenty-first year, Han and Qin jointly attacked Chu; they defeated the Chu general Qu Gai and beheaded 80,000 at Danyang. In this year, King Xuanhui died; Crown Prince Cang ascended the throne and is known as King Xiang.

## 24

In the fourth year of King Xiang's reign, he met with King Wu of Qin at Linjin. That autumn, Qin sent Gan Mao to attack our Yiyang. In the fifth year, Qin captured our Yiyang and beheaded 60,000 of us. King Wu of Qin died. In the sixth year, Qin returned Wusui to us once again. In the ninth year, Qin recaptured Wusui from us. In the tenth year, Crown Prince Ying paid a visit to Qin and returned. In the eleventh year, Qin attacked us and captured Rang. We joined forces with Qin to attack Chu and defeated the Chu general Tang Mei.

## 25

In the twelfth year, Crown Prince Ying died. Prince Gou and Gongzi Jushi vied for the position of crown prince. At that time, Jushi was a hostage in Chu. Su Dai said to Han Gou: "Jushi has fled and is now in Chu; King of Chu very much wants to bring him back. "Now, more than 100,000 Chu troops are stationed outside Fangcheng. Why don't you have King of Chu build a city with ten thousand houses near Yongshi? Han will surely raise an army to rescue it, and you will certainly be the commander." "You can then use the armies of Han and Chu to escort Jushi back into the country. The king will certainly listen to you, and he must bestow fiefs from both Chu and Han upon you." Han Gou followed his plan.

## 26

Chu besieged Yongshi, and Han sought aid from Qin. Qin had not yet mobilized forces and thus sent Gongsun Mei into Han (to intervene). Gongzhong asked, "Do you think Qin will come to rescue Han?" He replied: "King of Qin said, 'Please take the route through Nanzheng and Lantian to send troops into Chu in order to await you,' which seems unlikely." Gongzhong asked: "Do you really think it will happen?" He replied, "King of Qin must be following the old strategy of Zhang Yi. "When King Wei of Chu attacked Liang, Zhang Yi told the King of Qin: 'If we join forces with Chu to attack Wei, Wei will be defeated and submit to Chu. Since Han is naturally an ally of Wei, this would leave Qin isolated." "It would be better for us to send troops to observe the situation. When Wei and Chu engage in a major battle, Qin can seize the area west of the He River and return it.'" "Now his public declaration is that he supports Han, but in reality, he secretly favors Chu." "If you wait for Qin to arrive and then fight lightly with Chu, "Chu will take advantage of the fact that Qin is not actually committed, and will surely treat you as an easy opponent to resist." "If you defeat Chu in battle, they will then join forces with you against Chu, cross the Three Rivers, and return home." "But if you fail to defeat Chu, Chu will block the Three Rivers and defend them; you will be unable to rescue yourself." "I secretly fear for your safety." "Simaguang Geng returned to Ying three times, and Gan Mao met with Zhao Yu at Shangyu; their words about retrieving the seal actually suggest a secret agreement." Gongzhong was alarmed and asked, "If that is so, then what should we do?" He said: "You must prioritize Han over Qin, and yourself over Zhang Yi. "You would be better off quickly uniting your state with Qi and Chu; Qi and Chu will surely entrust their states to you." "What you truly dislike is Zhang Yi, but in reality, Qin cannot be entirely disregarded either." Thus, Chu lifted the siege of Yongshi.

## 27

Su Dai then said to Mi Rong, the younger brother of Queen Dowager of Qin: "Gongsu Boying is afraid that Qin and Chu will bring Jushi back into their country. Why don't you request a hostage from Han as a guarantee from Chu?" "If King of Chu agrees to send a hostage into Han, then Gongsu Boying will know that Qin and Chu do not intend to make Jushi an issue, and he will certainly align Han with Qin and Chu." "Qin and Chu would then use Han to pressure Wei; the House of Wei will dare not ally with Qi, thus leaving Qi isolated." "You also request a hostage from Chu for Qin. If Chu refuses, resentment will be directed toward Han." Han will coerce Qi and Wei into surrounding Chu; as a result, Chu will surely hold you in high esteem. "You can leverage the influence of Qin and Chu to accumulate virtue with Han, and Gongsu Boying must certainly treat you as his equal." Thus, Jushi ultimately failed to return to Han. Han appointed Gou as crown prince. The kings of Qi and Wei came.

## 28

In the fourteenth year, Han joined forces with the kings of Qi and Wei to attack Qin; they advanced as far as Hangu Pass and stationed their armies there. In the sixteenth year, Qin ceded the area outside the Yellow River and Wusui to us. King Xiang died; Crown Prince Gou ascended the throne, known as King Li.

## 29

In the third year of King Li's reign, he sent Gongsun Xi to lead forces from Zhou and Wei in an attack on Qin. Qin defeated our 240,000 troops and captured Gongsun Xi at Yique. In the fifth year, Qin seized our Wan. In the sixth year, we ceded 200 li of Wusui territory to Qin. In the tenth year, Qin defeated our army at Xiashan. In the twelfth year, he met with King Zhao of Qin in Xizhou and assisted Qin in attacking Qi. Qi was defeated, and King Min fled into exile. In the fourteenth year, he met with Qin between the Two Zhou states. In the twenty-first year, he sent Bao Juan to rescue Wei; they were defeated by Qin, and Juan fled to Kaifeng.

## 30

In the twenty-third year, Zhao and Wei attacked our Huayang. Han urgently sought aid from Qin, but Qin did not come to its rescue. The Prime Minister of Han said to Chen Shi: "The situation is urgent; I hope you, despite your illness, will make a one-night journey." Chen Shi met with Rang Hou. Rang Hou asked, "Is the situation urgent?" "That is why you have sent me here." Chen Shi replied, "It is not yet urgent." Rang Hou angrily said, "Is this how you serve as an envoy for your lord?" "Carriages and hats are in sight of each other; our small state has been urgently informed. Yet you say it is not urgent—why?" Chen Shi said, "If Han becomes desperate, they may change and seek alliances elsewhere. Since the situation is not yet critical, I have returned to report." Rang Hou said, "You need not see the king; please allow me to dispatch troops to rescue Han now." Within eight days, they arrived and defeated Zhao and Wei beneath Huayang. In this year, King Li died; his son Huanhui Wang ascended the throne.

## 31

In the first year of King Huanhui's reign, Han launched an attack on Yan. In the ninth year, Qin captured Xing from us and built a city along Fen River. In the tenth year, Qin attacked us at Taihang; our Shangdang Commandery governor surrendered the entire Shangdang region to Zhao. In the fourteenth year, Qin captured Sui's Shangdang from Zhao and killed more than 400,000 Ma Fu zi troops at Changping. In the seventeenth year, Qin captured our Yangcheng and Fusu. In the twenty-second year, King Zhao of Qin died. In the twenty-fourth year, Qin captured our Chenggao and Xingyang. In the twenty-sixth year, Qin completely captured our Shangdang. In the twenty-ninth year, Qin captured thirteen of our cities.

## 32

In the thirty-fourth year, King Huanhui died; his son Wang An was enthroned.

## 33

In the fifth year of King Wangan's reign, Qin attacked Han; in a desperate situation, Han sent Han Fei as an envoy to Qin. Qin detained Han Fei and eventually killed him.

## 34

In the ninth year, Qin took King Wangan captive and incorporated all its territory into Yingzhou Commandery. Thus Han perished.

## 35

Grand Historian said: The moving of Jin Jinggong by Han Jue, and the continuation of Zhao Ku's son Wu to fulfill Cheng Ying and Gongsun Chujiu's righteousness—this is a hidden virtue in the world. The achievements of the Han clan, though notable for their role in Jin, were not yet seen to be great. Yet they remained feudal lords alongside Zhao and Wei for more than ten generations; was this not fitting!
